Most Likely Scenario

Validating Garage Feasibility

What a buyer will need to confirm before committing to a design on the existing podium.

01

Structural Fit

Confirm the column grid, cores and foundation capacity support the buyer’s tower. The design basis is 27–29 stories; an equal or shorter tower on the same grid should work.

02

Condition After Three Years

Update the Partner report and assign it to the buyer. After three years exposed, the update should cover concrete, reinforcing and post-tensioning anchorages.

03

Drawings

Secure rights to the design drawings held under Kast’s design-build contract, so the buyer’s architect can build on the existing design rather than re-survey it.

04

Approvals & Code

A changed design needs downtown design approval, and any re-permitting will be under the new Florida Building Code.

05

The Church

Prior plans preserved the 1922 church as an event hall. A new design will need to account for it in the same way.

What the Buyer Gains

By purchasing this semi-completed parking structure, the buyer gains ~$19M worth of existing structure & foundations while avoiding an estimated $2.5M–$5M in demolition costs and roughly one year on the development schedule compared to a total rebuild.

Pro Forma Notes

This is a broker’s opinion of value prepared for the Receiver’s evaluation of sale alternatives. It is not an appraisal and should not be relied upon as one.

1. Gross Potential Rent

Pro Forma rents reflect an analysis of comparable Downtown Tampa high-rise product, applied to a proposed 312-unit mix across 325K rentable SF (1,042 SF average). Franklin Street assumes average rents of $4,356 /mo., or $4.18 PSF.

2. Operating Expenses

Franklin Street assumed an expense margin of 37%, in-line with current buyer underwriting

3. Cost to Complete

Franklin Street assumes $450,000 per unit, or $431 PSF to complete construction, in-line with today’s basis for new high-rise development.

4. Net Value of Completed Parking

The cost to complete above is priced as though the full parking program were built new. 262 of the planned spaces are already structurally complete within the existing parking deck, so that work is credited back rather than charged to a buyer twice.

At a blended below- and above-grade replacement cost of $50,000 per space, the completed spaces are valued at $13,100,000, reducing net cost to complete to $127,112,234, or $407,411 per unit. The remaining 238 spaces carry an estimated $11,900,000 of cost. At 500 total spaces the program parks at 1.60 spaces per unit.
